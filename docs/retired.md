# Retired approaches

Methods that were tried or worked out and are not pursued, each with the reason. Short by
design; the details are in the linked notes. Add an entry when something is removed or
abandoned.

| approach | tried | why it was retired | details |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MLDA** (multilevel delayed acceptance: coarse chains, fine details kept and perturbed, delayed acceptance) | toy and full model, until 2026-11-03; code removed in the legacy removal (bbbb97c) | fine-level acceptance ≈ 0: near an LP vertex the fine conditional's support and volume are set by the coarse state, so the conditionals at two coarse states barely overlap, and the overlap shrinks multiplicatively with the number of cells | [archive/toy_mlda.md](archive/toy_mlda.md) §5, [archive/setup_overview.md](archive/setup_overview.md) |
| **Non-equilibrium candidate moves** (NCMC: switch a coarse or capacity coordinate in stages with relaxation sweeps, accept by the work) | toy (2026-11-03), capacity moves on the 40-node model (2026-11-04, estimate) | on the toy only the first stage helped, and that stage is just a transported Metropolis move; global moves were never accepted under congestion. On the model a 1 % renewable change needs about 2500 increments (operation sweeps) because the force is rough: the force must be smoothed by an exact compensation (`kop`), not averaged | [archive/overnight_results.md](archive/overnight_results.md) §1, [archive/alternatives.md](archive/alternatives.md) §8, [mixing_diagnosis.md](mixing_diagnosis.md) §4.3 |
| **HMC** (reflective, on the regularized ensemble) | level 0, until 2026-11-03; removed (bbbb97c) | step size collapsed on the kinks of the piecewise-linear cost (ε → 1e-5); capacities and weather nearly frozen; slower than exact line Gibbs moves | [archive/implementation.md](archive/implementation.md) §5.2 and the results table |
| **Hard walls** (constraints as hard limits instead of priced violations) | until 2026-11-03; removed (bbbb97c) | the supports of the conditionals are set by the other coordinates, so moves stall at the walls (on the toy, staged moves did not help at all with hard walls); regularized (priced) constraints keep every line draw smooth and proper | [archive/plan.md](archive/plan.md), [archive/toy_results.md](archive/toy_results.md) |
| **Gibbs fine kernels, slice pair moves, temporal block moves, temporal multigrid** | until 2026-11-03; removed (bbbb97c) | superseded by the exact line moves of the local, flow and adaptive kernels and the storage multiscale sweep (no separate measured failure recorded) | commit 90f9aef, [archive/implementation.md](archive/implementation.md) |
| **LP storage form, renewables with utilization fields, decomposed LP start, Laplace basis** | until 2026-11-03; removed (bbbb97c) | superseded by the state-of-charge storage form, renewables as negative load (no confined utilization volume), the heuristic start and the graph-Haar basis | commit 90f9aef, [archive/physical_formulation.md](archive/physical_formulation.md) |
| **LP-linearized formulation** (the LP's inequalities sampled directly) | early design | the linearizations (capacity products, idle margins) create entropic and confinement artefacts in a sampled ensemble; replaced by the physical formulation | [archive/physical_formulation.md](archive/physical_formulation.md), [archive/sampling_formulations.md](archive/sampling_formulations.md) |
| **Capacity transport pair** (move capacity between nodes with the power flow following) | prototype, 2026-11-04 | median step 2.5 MW, no better than `kscale` | [mixing_diagnosis.md](mixing_diagnosis.md) §4.3 |
| **Regime-only moves** (non-centered regime block moves, centered FFBS at the fine level) | prototype, 2026-11-04 | regimes are practically a function of z0 (0.1–0.5 % uncertainty per day under the prior); proposals cost 5–9e4 nats and were never accepted; regime mixing is z0 mixing | [mixing_diagnosis.md](mixing_diagnosis.md) §5.3 |
| **Parallel tempering with a shared learned (MLP) bias and post-correction** | analysis, 2026-11-04 (not built) | replica exchange improves mixing but not the cold rung's bias error; pooling and an MLP reduce the model-class error, not the statistical floor (σ/√N); the post-correction weights compare two fits, not a fit with the truth; a β_v ladder would need thousands of rungs | [experiments.md](experiments.md) E16 |
